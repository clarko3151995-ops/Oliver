# Felt Diorama Studio

A single-page app (`index.html`, no build step) that draws handmade, mixed-media
dioramas: cut-out pieces of felt, card, wood, fabric, glass, metal, cork, stone,
found nature and recycled bits, stacked with drop shadows and finished with
stitching, buttons, pins, tape and yarn.

Open `craft/index.html` in any modern browser.

## Materials
Pick a **Material style** (Mixed media, Felt & cardboard, Nature table, Junk drawer,
Fabric patchwork, Workshop) or tick material families yourself. Each part of the
scene only uses materials that suit it, so grass can be felt, moss, gingham or
tissue paper, but never marble.

| Family | Materials |
|---|---|
| Felt | felt, heathered wool felt |
| Paper & card | cardstock, kraft, watercolour, speckled, corrugated card, ribbed card, exposed flutes |
| Wood | birch, walnut |
| Fabric | burlap, canvas, denim, plaid, corduroy, gingham, lace, jacquard ribbon |
| Glass | clear, frosted, sea glass, bubble glass, iridescent, crackle |
| Metal | brushed, galvanized, copper patina, rust, brass, corrugated tin, perforated |
| Cork & foam | cork, chunky cork, sponge, foam, terracotta |
| Stone | sandstone, slate, granite, marble, lava, pebbles |
| Nature | moss, bark, sand, leaf litter, pressed leaves and flowers, twigs, jute twine |
| Recycled | newspaper, tissue, foil, bubble wrap, egg carton, bottle caps, paper clips, masking tape |
| Buttons & notions | wooden and plastic buttons, snaps, eyelets, safety pins, push pins, embroidery floss, washi tape |

## Modes
- **Island**: makes a floating island from a seed. Same seed, same island.
  Pick a palette (Autumn, Spring, Winter, Twilight), set how many trees, and
  turn falling leaves, backdrop hills, stitching and the craft frame on or off.
- **Photo**: turns your photo into stacked cutouts. It sorts the colours into
  3 to 12 shades, gives each one a hand-cut edge and a material from the chosen
  style, and stacks them with shadows. Pick a file, drop or paste one, or use the
  built-in sample. The photo stays on your device.

**Save PNG** downloads the picture at 1600×1100.

## How the look is made
| Element | Technique |
|---|---|
| Felt | Tiled texture of ~5,000 short curly fibres over soft mottling and speckle; fuzzy fibres along every cut edge; 2–5 px darker "thickness" swept under the piece |
| Cardboard | Kraft face with flecks and grain; the edge is a swept band of vertical flute stripes (the corrugation) |
| Diorama depth | Every piece casts its own blurred drop shadow, sized by how far it is lifted off the layer below |
| Hand-cut shapes | Ellipses wobbled by a few sine harmonics, plus a little scissor jitter |
| Stitching | Dashed thread line set in from the edge, with a tiny shadow |
| Haberdashery | Wooden buttons (grain rings, 4 holes, thread cross), twisted-ply yarn, yarn ball |
| Paper | Layered noise, faceted crease shading, crease lines, vignette |
