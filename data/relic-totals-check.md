# Relic totals check (my reading of owned relic cards vs the in-game Relic Stats)

Method: add the effect values on every owned relic card I have read (+20% for displayed ones) and compare with the "Relics" source column on the character stats screen. Flat stats (ATK, HP, DEF, SPD) were not re-added; only stats where each owned relic is rare enough to track by hand.

Conclusions
- Set bonuses are NOT included in the relic totals (Crit Rate matches exactly without them).
- The flat/percent effects I transcribed are accurate for the main stats.
- Small gaps (DMG RES 3%, Physical Mastery/RES ~2K) probably come from owned relics whose cards I have not transcribed: the 11 owned "Other" tab (event) relics and parts of the Cinder Ridge Rare tier.
- Dark Affinity within 1.5%: reading is complete enough to rank Dark relics.
